196,726 words from ESDB (formerly SCOWL), size 80, with American and British (-ise and -ize) spellings, variant levels 0 through 5, and inflections. Pinned revision: 1e5b7d3a72f47a71da5d28686c1dd4b397178485.

Only ASCII lowercase words with lowercase base forms are included. Entries marked as names, possible names, abbreviations, trademarks, nonwords, affixes, or multiword parts are excluded. Source words are never blindly lowercased. Common words that are also names, such as rose, mark, will, and bill, retain their independent common-word entries. Apostrophes, hyphens, other punctuation, and accented spellings are excluded.

Size 80 includes uncommon words useful in word games, adding 63,722 entries over our original size-70 list. Size 85's additional older or obsolete entries are not included.

This is a spelling database, not every English word: words beyond size 80 are omitted, and upstream classification can contain mistakes. Capitalized-only words, including some adjectives, are conservatively excluded. Inflections must exist in the database; the app does not invent plurals or verb forms. Some words may be informal, offensive, or adult language.
